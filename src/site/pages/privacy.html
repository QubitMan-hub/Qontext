Privacy: what this site keeps about you

No cookies, no analytics, no accounts, no ads. Nobody is tracked, and nothing you do here is sent anywhere.

What stays on your device

  • The number of your world, in session storage, so the site stays the same world while the tab is open. It goes away when you close the tab.
  • Your Phase Dial angle, in local storage, once you turn the dial. Follow system clears it.
  • In the Atelier, your current project, your saved palettes and the last tab you had open, in local storage, so your work is there next time.

All of it stays in your browser. Clearing this site's data in your browser settings removes it.

Who else sees a visit

  • The host. Cloudflare serves these pages, and like any web host it handles your IP address and browser details to deliver them and to keep attacks out. Cloudflare's analytics are not turned on. Cloudflare's privacy policy.
  • The fonts. The typefaces come from Google Fonts, so your browser asks Google's servers for them, and Google sees your IP address and browser details for that request. Google's privacy policy.
  • GitHub, if you follow a link to the source code. GitHub's privacy statement.

The film comes from this site itself. Its poster and the first few bytes load when you scroll near it, and the rest when you press play.

Probe

Probe runs on your own computer. If you run circuits on IBM Quantum, your IBM token stays on your computer and is sent only to IBM.
