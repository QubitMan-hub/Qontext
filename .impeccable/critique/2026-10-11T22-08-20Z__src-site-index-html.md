### Priority issues
P1. Nine rooms, one template. Every room is a strip, a meta line, a giant name, copy on the left, a white card exhibit on the right, then "What it's for" with diamond bullets. By the third room people scroll instead of read. Fix: let each room's physics set its layout. Spooky Pair splits the page in two with the thread crossing the gutter. Fringe runs edge to edge. Mermin's Dare is a board in the middle with almost no copy. Commands: layout, bolder.

P1. The ending is a stock startup page. Firsts (6 cards) and Tools (7 cards) could sit on any SaaS site, and "Things this page does that we have not seen any website do" grades its own homework. Fix: move each First into the room where it happens as a margin note, and turn Tools into a short index of objects you can open. Commands: distill, layout.

P2. Scaffolding repeats. The "What it's for" lists, meta lines and strips appear nine times. Fix: one plain sentence of use per room, inside the copy. Commands: distill, clarify.

P2. One loud voice. Heavy Anybody carries every big moment, so nothing outranks anything. Instrument Sans is one of the most common faces on generated sites. Fix: use Anybody's width axis for character (tall narrow wall labels, wide hero) and give reading text a less common face. Command: typeset.

P3. Copy. Four "X. No Y." aphorisms, for example "One tile can hold two true colors. No ordinary palette can do that." Fix: say it once, plainly. Command: clarify.

P3. Motion. The collapse uses an overshoot curve that bounces every tile. A measurement should snap and settle. Command: animate.

P3. Small things. Tile labels at 9 to 10 px in the two grids. The film poster is an empty image until the script runs.
