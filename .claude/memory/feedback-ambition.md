Aim for work nobody has done before, finished to production quality. When offering choices, include at least
one genuinely new idea, not only the conventional option (light and dark mode was rejected as "something all
websites have"; the Phase dial was chosen instead).

**Why:** the owner has asked repeatedly for "crazy" features, "do your literal best", "don't compromise", and
for Qontext to be "the best product out there".

**How to apply:** ambition applies to product ideas and finish. Code stays lean (Ponytail's smallest complete
change), and every claim of done is backed by tests, the perf budget and screenshots.
